What this is, and what it refuses to be

No guarantees

Nothing here guarantees success, and any site that tells you otherwise is selling something. What a program can honestly do is put your hours against a real syllabus, with feedback loops and gates that make self-deception difficult.

Scoped against your hours

Four weeks at five hours a week is eighty hours. That is a real amount of training and it is not professional competence in anything. The planner computes what your budget actually reaches and says so before you start.

Drills, not reading lists

Every phase prescribes named drills with doses and protocols, a weekly session structure, and a gate with criteria another person could verify. Reading is capped at a quarter of your time.

01

The disciplines

Thirteen skills selected on one criterion: they compound. Each converts into income, autonomy or judgment, and each keeps paying long after the program ends. Choose one — training two at once halves the rate of progress in both.

Hour figures are estimates of deliberate practice — focused work at the edge of your ability with feedback — not time spent in the vicinity of the subject.

The five rules every program enforces

01

Practice at the edge, not in the comfortable middle

Sessions target a 50–85% success rate. Above that band you are performing; below it you are flailing. Difficulty is adjusted, not endured.

02

Input is capped at a quarter of your time

Reading, courses and videos are the most comfortable and least productive part of skill acquisition. Every program caps acquisition sessions and forces production.

03

Every phase ends at a gate

Gates have criteria another person could verify. You do not advance on time served; you advance on evidence.

04

Feedback must come from outside your own head

Each discipline names its arena — where real signal lives. Practice without an arena plateaus quickly and invisibly.

05

Consolidation is scheduled, not earned

Programs of ten weeks or more include reduced-load weeks. Recovery taken before it is needed prevents the collapse that ends most programs at week seven.

04

The daily dispatch

One passage each day from a tradition you choose — Stoicism, Taoism and Zen, strategy, mastery, meaning, or wealth and leverage. Each comes with what it actually means, one concrete practice for today, and an honest counterweight, because philosophy applied without tension becomes a slogan.
